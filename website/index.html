Everything that needs you, in one window.

Warsha is a Mac app for developers with too much going on. Your pull requests with their real merge state, the Linear tickets behind them, today's meetings and your own notes, ranked by what to do next.

Four things you check all day, together

Pull requests, honestly

Only required checks count as failing, and Warsha shows who actually blocks the merge under your branch rules. Conflicts and branches behind the base are flagged before you find out the hard way.

Meetings in context

Your Google Calendar beside your work: one click to join, and a heads-up when something is moved, cancelled or added since you last looked.

Tickets next to their code

Linear tickets sit with the PRs that close them. Up next ranks what's assigned to you, and New for you gathers mentions, comments and assignments until you mark them seen.

Your own notes

Pin anything, keep a note on a PR or ticket, add todos with due dates, and park what's waiting on someone else, with a reason it can post for you.

  • Activity A week of what you shipped, reviewed and closed, ready for standup.
  • Worktrees Your clones and their git worktrees, with what's uncommitted in each.
  • Menu bar What needs you, a global shortcut and notifications, with the window closed.
  • Hand off to Claude A ready-made prompt to resolve a PR's merge conflicts in Claude Code.

Runs on your Mac, and only there

There's no Warsha server and no account. The app talks straight to GitHub, Linear and Google, keeps your sign-ins in the macOS Keychain and its own data in a database on your Mac. Calendar access is read-only.

Install

  1. Download the latest Warsha-x.y.z.zip from Releases and open it.
  2. Drag Warsha into your Applications folder and open it.
  3. Warsha isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS stops it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Got the GitHub CLI? This skips the warning: gh release download --repo ahmedmahmoudo/warsha --pattern 'Warsha-*.zip' --dir /tmp/warsha --clobber && ditto -x -k /tmp/warsha/Warsha-*.zip ~/Applications && open ~/Applications/Warsha.app
